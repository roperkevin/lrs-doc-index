       How to enter UC Charge Codes into PS Kimble Time Keeping
Select Esri Conference from Drop down list

                                                           Select _UC from Time Activity Categories

Copy & paste in charge number or manually enter in the charge number . Major Charge Numbers below; others are found at UC Charging Link: https://compass.esri.com/org/marketing/UC/ logistics/Pages/charge-codes.aspx

Pre-UC Charge Number: C42019M1100
During & Post UC Charge Number: C42019M1000
Travel Time: C42019M2310
Attendance - All Sessions: C42019M2200
Attendance - Meetings and Customer Account Management: C42019M2100
