## Demo Day Checklists

| pre-demo |  |
| --- | --- |
| Logistical | Arrive early<br>Locate room and setup, aim ready 15-30 mins before if possible<br>Turn off cell phone - ringer AND vibrate<br>Put a demo sign on your door if virtual |
| Personal | Perform warm up activities<br>Restroom break<br>Make sure not hungry<br>Water with you<br>No gum or candy in your mouth!<br>Don’t drink soda/pop before presentation |
| Equipment | Fresh restart<br>Check resolution<br>Check your headset/microphone<br>Open ArcGIS apps in advance<br>Disable messengers<br>Turn off email |

| delivery |  |
| --- | --- |
| You | Body position<br>Strong voice<br>Pauses<br>Eye contact<br>Mouse control – take your hand off |
| Questions | Repeat, Answer, verify<br>Manage number and timing<br>Admit you don’t know<br>Follow up later |
| If the demo fails… | 1. Acknowledge that you ran into a bug<br>2. Describe what should have happened.<br>3. Pick up at the point directly after the bug if possible |
