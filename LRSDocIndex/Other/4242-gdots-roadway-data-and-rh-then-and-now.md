## Summary

This document reviews the history and implementation of the Georgia Department of Transportation's (GDOT) Roadway Data and Roads and Highways (RnH) system. It covers data collection methods, challenges faced during early adoption, the involvement of the consulting firm Arcadis, and subsequent improvements in knowledge, policy, and processes within the Office of Transportation Data.

    • Once solution to problem was determined, IT acquired the software

Official Migration to Roads and Highways!
• All data migrated to RnH on May 23, 2015
• Created a “legacy” date for all of the data imported into the application
• Transferred all data: good or bad
• Date carries importance toward updating old and managing new data
• Threshold for identifying issues

2015-2019: Years In the Land of the Unknown
• After training the team: considerations were made:
   • Time spent/effectiveness
   • self taught/ trial by fire
   • creation of workarounds
   • occasional outside help

End result: Great intentions, good effort, okay results.

                   The Arrival of Arcadis
 Time to ask for help!
• Researched firms to determine who could best assist
 • Brought in Arcadis for expertise!
• Lots of knowledge regarding proper usage of RnH and readily available to help

                                Down To Business
          • Interviewing the Staff
             • Once aboard, interviews ensued

                          Knowledge Issues
•   Discovered that team knew a lot but didn’t totally understand certain themes that made RnH tick: i.e.
    Temporality
•   Edits made out of sequence affected the accuracy of data
•   Learning significance of effective dates vs. open to traffic dates

                            Policy Issues
• OTD doesn’t “own” data
• All policy decisions had to go through IT
• Decisions made on high levels without consideration for day to day users.

                  And the results are….
• Approximately ten (10) interviews (and many bouts of anxiety) later recommendations were written
• Several impromptu training classes held in the interim
• Wrote recommendations for both knowledge/process improvement and policy implementation
• Prioritization of recommendations
