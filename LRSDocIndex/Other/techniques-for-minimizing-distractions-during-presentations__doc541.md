## Summary

This document provides practical techniques to reduce distractions during presentations, including managing smooth transitions, minimizing typing, preparing desktop and browser environments, and using screen space effectively. It also covers audience management, good cartography practices, technical knowledge about projectors, and backup plans for software or hardware failures. Emphasis is placed on thorough preparation, emergency kits, and practicing presentation delivery for clarity and confidence.
