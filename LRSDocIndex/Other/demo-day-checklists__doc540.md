## Demo Day Checklists

| pre-demo |  |
| --- | --- |
| Logistical | Arrive early Locate room and setup, aim ready 15-30 mins before if possible Turn off cell phone - ringer AND vibrate Put a demo sign on your door if virtual |
| Personal | Perform warm up activities Restroom break Make sure not hungry Water with you No gum or candy in your mouth! Don’t drink soda/pop before presentation |
| Equipment | Fresh restart Check resolution Check your headset/microphone Open ArcGIS apps in advance Disable messengers Turn off email |

| delivery |  |
| --- | --- |
| You | Body position Strong voice Pauses Eye contact Mouse control – take your hand off |
| Questions | Repeat, Answer, verify Manage number and timing Admit you don’t know Follow up later |
| If the demo fails… | 1. Acknowledge that you ran into a bug 2. Describe what should have happened. 3. Pick up at the point directly after the bug if possible |
