## Slide 2 — Do’s and Don'ts

- Take notes into ONE NOTE (Debalin to set up)
- No compulsion to answer at that moment, you can always get back
- Ask them about their use cases
- Be aware of your meeting time, you can meet again if time permits
- Use ESRI provided desktop background
- Don’t commit to any new tool or feature
- Don’t commit to the timeline even if the item is in the backlog
- Don’t work on their data, just suggest
